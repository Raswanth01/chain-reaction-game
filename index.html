CHAIN REACTOR

Players

RULES

► SYSTEM OBJECTIVE

The Player must eliminate all opponents by capturing every cell on the grid. The session terminates instantly when a single Player remains, or when the Global Clock reaches zero—in which case The Player with the highest score is granted supremacy.

► DEPLOYMENT PROTOCOL

1. ACTION PHASE: During the 20-second turn window, The Player may either Place a Dot or Execute a Power-Up. Performing either action successfully concludes the turn.

2. INITIALIZATION: The very first move functions as a "Jumpstart". It instantly populates a cell to [Capacity - 1]. (e.g., A Center cell with Cap 4 will initialize with 3 dots upon the first click).

3. STANDARD GROWTH: Following initialization, each subsequent placement adds exactly one dot to controlled or uncaptured cells.

4. DETONATION: Cells reaching critical capacity explode, dispersing dots to orthogonal neighbors and flipping ownership to The Player who triggered the reaction.

► HACKER OVERRIDES

5. PORTAL WARP: In Hacker Mode, specific nodes are linked. Explosions entering a portal are instantly re-routed to the corresponding exit coordinate.

► POWER-UPS

6. KINETIC BOMB: Awarded upon achieving a 5-step chain reaction. Once deployed, it initiates a high-impact surge that clears all dots from the target cell and its four immediate neighbors (North, South, East, West).

7. EMPIRE SWAP: The ultimate strategic heist. Unlocked through a 7-step chain reaction and a 250-point data score. Allows The Player to instantly trade an entire controlled territory with a targeted opponent, flipping the balance of the grid.

► WIN CONDITIONS

8. TOTAL WIPEOUT: Instant victory is achieved by capturing every cell on the grid or by eliminating all opponents.

9. SYSTEM TIMEOUT: When the clock hits 00:00, The Player with the highest score is declared the winner.

10. THE DRAW: If the countdown ends and multiple Players hold the exact same score, the match is officially recorded as a DRAW among the players with the high score.

11. SURVIVAL: If all other opponents are eliminated or their turn-timers expire, the last player standing on the grid wins by default.

► SCORING SYSTEM

12. DEPLOYMENT POINTS: The Player earns +1 point for every manual dot placement on the grid.

13. EXPLOSION YIELD: When a cell detonates, The Player earns +1 point for every dot that successfully transfers into a neighboring cell. Large chain reactions cause scores to spike rapidly as data spreads across the board.

► ELIMINATION PROTOCOLS

14. TOTAL WIPEOUT: A Player is officially eliminated when controlling zero cells. Note: Elimination cannot occur until The Player has completed the Initialization Move.

15. TURN TIMEOUT: If a Player’s 20-second turn clock reaches zero, that Player is flagged as Inactive. Current dots remain on the board as "Frozen Assets" for rivals to capture, but the Inactive Player can no longer take turns.

CRITICAL WARNING: The Player must exercise extreme caution during deployment. Any chain reactions still processing after the 20-second window expires will result in Elimination of the player. Strategy must account for resolution time.
