CHAIN REACTOR

RULES

► SYSTEM OBJECTIVE

Eliminate all opponents by capturing every cell on the grid. The game ends instantly if you are the last player standing, or when the Global Clock hits zero—in which case the player with the highest score wins.

► DEPLOYMENT PROTOCOL

1. ACTION PHASE: On your turn of 20 seconds, you may either Place a Dot or Execute a Power-Up. Performing either action successfully ends your turn.

2. INITIALIZATION: Your very first move is a "Jumpstart". It instantly fills a cell to [Capacity - 1]. (e.g., A Center cell with Cap 4 will start with 3 dots on your first click).

3. STANDARD GROWTH: After your first move, each subsequent click adds exactly one dot to your controlled cells or uncaptured cells.

4. DETONATION: Cells reaching capacity explode, sending dots to neighbors/orthagonal cells and flipping their ownership.

► HACKER OVERRIDES

5. PORTAL WARP: In Hacker Mode, specific cells are linked. Explosions hitting a portal are instantly re-routed to the linked exit coordinate.

► POWER-UPS

6. KINETIC BOMB: Awarded upon achieving a 5-step chain reaction. Once deployed, it initiates a high-impact surge that clears all dots from the target cell and its four immediate neighbors (North, South, East, West).

7. EMPIRE SWAP: The ultimate strategic heist. Unlocked through a 7-step chain reaction and a 250 data score. Allows you to instantly trade your entire territory with a targeted opponent, flipping the balance of the grid.

► WIN CONDITIONS

8. TOTAL WIPEOUT: You win instantly if you capture every cell on the grid or by eliminating all opponents.

9. SYSTEM TIMEOUT: When the clock hits 00:00, the player with the highest score is declared the winner.

10. THE DRAW: If the countdown ends and two or more players have the exact same score, the match is officially recorded as a DRAW.

11. SURVIVAL: If all other opponents are eliminated or their turn-timers expire, you win by being the last active process on the grid.

► SCORING SYSTEM

12. DEPLOYMENT POINTS: You earn +1 point every time you manually place a dot on the grid.

13. EXPLOSION YIELD: When a cell reaches critical mass and detonates, you earn +1 point for every dot that successfully transfers into a neighboring cell. Large chain reactions will cause your score to spike rapidly as dots spread across the board.
